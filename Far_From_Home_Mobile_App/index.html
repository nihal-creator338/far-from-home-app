📱 SIM card guidance

  1. Check the provider's current eligibility rules for international students.
  2. Ask which identity, visa and address documents are accepted.
  3. Visit an authorized service centre and complete KYC in person.
  4. Keep your receipt and verify activation before leaving.
Requirements vary by provider and immigration status. Verify with the provider before visiting.

🏦 Bank account & UPI guidance

  1. Ask the bank about account types available to international students.
  2. Confirm accepted passport, visa, address and university documents.
  3. Check minimum balance, fees and debit-card conditions.
  4. Ask the bank about UPI eligibility and activation steps.
Banking eligibility and KYC requirements are bank-specific. This is general guidance, not a guarantee of eligibility.

Help Centre

Quick answers, less confusion.

Simple starting points for common international student questions.

Where can I ask about FRRO requirements?

Contact LPU International Affairs / the designated university support desk and check the official e-FRRO portal. Requirements and deadlines depend on your case.

How do I request a Bonafide certificate?

Use the university's official UMS process and select the purpose required by the requesting authority. The demo request button only prepares a reminder.

How can I get a SIM card or bank account?

Check current eligibility and KYC requirements directly with an authorized telecom provider or bank. Bring only the documents they confirm are required.

How do I find suitable food?

Use the Taste of Home filters as a starting point, then confirm ingredients, preparation and dietary suitability directly with the provider.

How can I meet senior students?

Open Buddy Connect to try the demo request flow. A real matching service would require verified student profiles and availability.

What should I do in an emergency?

Use verified LPU security / University Hospital contact details or local emergency services. This prototype intentionally does not invent phone numbers.
